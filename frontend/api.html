API

Five endpoints, all GET

The viewer itself uses these. Every endpoint answers with JSON; positions are in decimal degrees (ICRS).

GET

/api/epochs

Lists the epochs at a position and the number of exposures per band.

ra, decposition in degrees
GET/api/epochs?ra=331.096&dec=-56.801
GET

/api/stack

One stacked image per epoch for one band. Images are base64 little-endian float32 in MJy/sr on a 3.075″ grid, row 0 at the south edge.

ra, deccentre of the view in degrees
fovfield of view in arcseconds, 30 to 600
detdetector band, 1 to 6
GET/api/stack?ra=331.096&dec=-56.801&fov=240&det=5
GET

/api/spectrum

One point per exposure at a spot inside the view: wavelength and bandwidth in µm, flux and noise in mJy, date, detector and epoch.

ra, dec, fovthe view, as for /api/stack
pra, pdecthe spot to measure, in degrees
aperaperture radius in native pixels, 1 to 3 (default 2)
GET/api/spectrum?ra=331.096&dec=-56.801&fov=240&pra=331.096&pdec=-56.801&aper=2
GET

/api/catalog

Catalogue sources in the view: position at the catalogue’s reference epoch, proper motion in mas/yr and its significance, and a magnitude (G for Gaia, W2 for CatWISE).

namegaia or catwise
ra, dec, fovthe view, as for /api/stack
GET/api/catalog?name=gaia&ra=331.096&dec=-56.801&fov=240
GET

/api/health

Reports that the service is running.

GET/api/health
